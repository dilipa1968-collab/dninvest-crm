Investment Value = sum of accounts tagged "Investment" on the Assets page (Nirmala Fd, Nirmala Mf, DILIP HUF, Dilip Mf, FD Idfc, Tata Aia) — you can fill it straight from the button. "Cash & Bank" is tracked separately (Bhagwan, Chery, Bank, Ghar cash, etc.), and is also included in Total Balance.
Total Invested So Far = last month's Total Invested + this month's New Investment (running total).
Investment Growth = this month's Investment Value − last month's Investment Value (market movement only) = the Dashboard's "Investment Growth" when it's the latest month.
Net Worth Growth = this month's Net Worth − last month's Net Worth (the full change — all accounts combined) = the Dashboard's "Net Worth Growth".
Cash Income = Net Worth Growth − Investment Growth + New Investment. This is the money that didn't come from investment market movement — i.e. salary/business/rent-style income minus expenses = the Dashboard's "Cash Growth".
