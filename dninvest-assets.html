Mahine-War Growth aur Income

Har mahine ke end mein 3 cheezein bharein: us mahine kitna naya paisa invest kiya, aapke investment ki current value, aur total net worth (ya "Sync" button se seedha upar wale Total Balance se le lein). Baaki sab column khud calculate ho jaate hain.
Mahine-War Table
Mahina Naya Invest Total Invest Ab Tak Investment Value Investment Growth Net Worth Net Worth Growth Baaki Income
Koi mahina add nahi hua — upar se ek add karein.
Investment Value = Assets page mein "Investment" category wale accounts (Nirmala Fd, Nirmala Mf, DILIP HUF, Dilip Mf, FD Idfc, Tata Aia) ka jod — button se seedha bhar sakte hain. "Cash & Bank" category alag se track hoti hai (Bhagwan, Chery, Bank, Ghar cash, etc.), Total Balance mein woh bhi shaamil hai.
Total Invest Ab Tak = pichle mahine ka Total Invest + is mahine ka Naya Invest (chalta hua jod).
Investment Growth = is mahine ki Investment Value − pichle mahine ki Investment Value (sirf market ka asar) = Dashboard ka "Investment Growth" jab latest mahina ho.
Net Worth Growth = is mahine ka Net Worth − pichle mahine ka Net Worth (poora badlaav — sab accounts milakar) = Dashboard ka "Net Worth Growth".
Baaki Income = Net Worth Growth − Investment Growth + Naya Invest. Yeh wo paisa hai jo investment ke market-movement se nahi aaya — matlab salary/business/rent jaisi income minus kharcha = Dashboard ka "Cash Growth".
