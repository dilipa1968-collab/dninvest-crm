Incentive rates & formula

Set once, applies to all employees. Changes reflect everywhere instantly.

Brokerage incentive formula

Inc.Salary = Basic Salary + Performance Incentive (set per RM on the Employees page). Formula: Brokerage below Lower×Inc.Salary → ₹0. Between Lower× and Upper×Inc.Salary → (Brokerage − Lower×Inc.Salary) × Tier 1%. At or above Upper×Inc.Salary → (Brokerage − Lower×Inc.Salary) × Tier 2% (Tier 2 is the total rate on the excess, not added on top of Tier 1).
