🔒 Call Date Lock (for RMs)

RM ke Follow-up form me Last Call aur Next Call date ki limit set karo. Isse pehle ya iske baad ki date RM daal nahi payega. Blank chhodo = us taraf koi limit nahi. (Admin par koi limit nahi lagti.)

✅ Rolling mode: limit har roz khud aage khisak jati hai — daily click karne ki zarurat nahi. Neeche aaj se kitne din ka gap allow karna hai wo daalo. Blank = us taraf koi limit nahi.
Example: -7 = 7 din pehle tak · 0 = aaj · 90 = aaj se 90 din baad tak.
